SECTION 1: RTGS CORE PRICING SCHEME
========================================================================

Two pricing options are available to RTGS DCA holders:

OPTION A
  Monthly fee per DCA .......................... EUR 400.00
  Fee per payment order (flat) ................. EUR 0.80

OPTION B
  Monthly fee per DCA .......................... EUR 5,000.00
  Volume-based payment order fees (cumulative degressive bands):

  Band | From      | To        | Unit Price (EUR)
  -----+-----------+-----------+-----------------
    1  |         1 |    10,000 | 0.60
    2  |    10,001 |    35,000 | 0.50
    3  |    35,001 |    80,000 | 0.40
    4  |    80,001 |   135,000 | 0.20
    5  |   135,001 |   200,000 | 0.125
    6  |   200,001 |   300,000 | 0.08
    7  |   300,001 |       ... | 0.05

BILLING GROUPS

A central bank may create a billing group. The group leader must
choose Option B for its RTGS DCA. Other members may choose Option A
for their DCA (which determines their DCA fixed fee only — not the
transaction fee computation). The RTGS payment orders of all members
in a billing group are aggregated for the purpose of computing
transaction fees in a degressive manner across the group. The billing
group leader's Option B tariff bands are used to derive the applicable
band prices.

The total transaction fee for the group is computed by applying the
degressive bands to the aggregate volume. A pro-rata unit price is
then derived by dividing the total group fee by the total group
volume. This unit price is applied to each member's individual
payment order volume to determine their payment order charge.

ROUNDING CONVENTION

The pro-rata unit price is carried to the fourth decimal place. At the
fifth decimal position, digits 0-4 cause the fourth decimal to be
retained (truncated), while digits 5-9 cause the fourth decimal to be
incremented. Each member's payment order charge (unit price multiplied
by volume) is then rounded to two decimal places using the same
convention. All invoice totals are rounded to the second decimal place.

SECTION 3: BIC REGISTRATION FEES (RTGS)
========================================================================

  Addressable BIC (correspondent) ...... EUR 20.00/month
  Unpublished BIC ...................... EUR 30.00/month
  Multi-addressee access ............... EUR 80.00/month

Fees are charged to the direct participant responsible for the
business relationship with these entities and that has registered them.

========================================================================
SECTION 4: RTGS LIQUIDITY TRANSFER PRICING
========================================================================

Liquidity transfers across different banking groups:
  Within RTGS .......................... EUR 0.80 per transfer
  From RTGS to CLM .................... EUR 0.80 per transfer

Liquidity transfers within the same banking group are free of charge.
AS-related liquidity transfers are charged under the AS pricing scheme.

========================================================================
SECTION 5: T2S CASH-RELATED SERVICES PRICING
========================================================================

Each completed service activity is counted and charged per unit:

  Activity Type                    | Unit Price (EUR)
  ---------------------------------+-----------------
  Messages bundled into a file     | 0.004
  Transmissions                    | 0.012
  U2A queries                      | 0.100
  A2A queries                      | 0.007
  A2A reports                      | 0.004
  Internal T2S liquidity transfer  | 0.141
  Intra-balance movements          | 0.094

Invoice total: sum all unrounded line-item amounts, then round the
total to the second decimal place.

SECTION 6: TIPS PRICING SCHEME
========================================================================

6.1 TIPS SETTLEMENT FEE

  Fee per instant payment order: EUR 0.002 total
  Split equally: EUR 0.001 originator, EUR 0.001 beneficiary

  Applies to: settled and unsettled instant payment orders,
  and settled and unsettled positive recall answers.

  Charged to both TIPS DCA holders (PSPs) and ASTA holders (ACHs).

6.2 PSP FIXED FEES

  Fixed fee per TIPS DCA .................... EUR 800.00/month
  BIC fee per authorised account user ....... EUR 20.00/month
    - First BIC per DCA is included free of charge
    - Maximum 50 chargeable BICs per DCA

6.3 ACH FIXED FEES

  Fixed fee per TIPS ASTA ................... EUR 3,000.00/month
  BIC fee per authorised account user ....... EUR 20.00/month
    - All BICs are chargeable from the 1st (no free BIC)
    - Maximum 50 chargeable BICs per ASTA

6.4 ACH INTERNAL SETTLEMENT FEE

  Monthly fee based on underlying gross volume of internally settled
  instant payments (cumulative degressive bands):

  Band | From         | To           | Unit Price (EUR)
  -----+--------------+--------------+-----------------
    A  |            1 |   10,000,000 | 0.00040
    B  |   10,000,001 |   25,000,000 | 0.00030
    C  |   25,000,001 |  100,000,000 | 0.00020
    D  |  100,000,001 |          ... | 0.00015
