FIELD VALIDATION REPORT
Scene: LC09_L2SP_017041_20230615 — Processing Quality Review
================================================================

The following discrepancies were observed between pipeline outputs and
independent validation data collected during concurrent field campaigns.

1. QUALITY MASKING GAPS

   Scene border fill pixels (corners of the acquisition footprint) are
   not flagged in the cloud mask and propagate as valid observations into
   all downstream products. These locations contain no valid Earth surface
   measurements and must be excluded.

   A cirrus-contaminated region in the lower-right portion of the scene
   (approximately rows 140-160, columns 140-160) confirmed by concurrent
   aircraft overflight is not being masked. Thin cirrus introduces
   additive path radiance that corrupts surface reflectance retrievals.

   Cloud buffer regions immediately adjacent to identified opaque cloud
   bodies are not being excluded. Partial cloud contamination in these
   transition zones degrades spectral index accuracy for pixels that
   border cloud features.

2. SURFACE REFLECTANCE CALIBRATION

   Portable field spectrometer measurements at a dense canopy validation
   site in the northwest quadrant recorded NIR surface reflectance of
   approximately 0.45 sr. The pipeline's calibrated reflectance values
   at the corresponding pixels read approximately 0.85 — nearly double
   the expected magnitude. This systematic overestimation is consistent
   across all spectral bands and surface types, suggesting an error in
   the digital-number-to-reflectance conversion rather than a
   band-specific sensor artifact. The multiplicative scale factor appears
   correct; the additive component warrants investigation.

3. DERIVED INDEX ACCURACY

   As a consequence of the calibration anomaly, NDVI values for dense
   forest canopy (approximately 0.31) are substantially lower than the
   0.80 expected from field-verified sites. Water body NDVI reads near
   zero rather than the expected moderately negative values. MNDWI
   dynamic range is similarly compressed — open water that should exhibit
   strongly positive MNDWI shows values near zero.

4. ZONAL STATISTICS

   The reported clear_pixel_count for zone 1 (northwest_forest) equals
   the total zone area despite this zone overlapping known cloud and
   cloud shadow features. The count should reflect only quality-assured
   uncontaminated pixels. Cross-referencing with the cloud mask confirms
   that contaminated pixels exist within this zone but are not being
   excluded from the count.

5. PRODUCT COMPLETENESS

   The geographic-coordinate (EPSG:4326) reprojection of the NDVI
   product required for web mapping service integration was not generated.
   This deliverable is required for the final data package.
