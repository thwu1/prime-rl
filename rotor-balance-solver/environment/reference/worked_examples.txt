WORKED EXAMPLES - BALANCE ANALYSIS

Example 1: Four-Plane Rotating Balance
---------------------------------------
Shaft with four planes. Known masses:
  Plane A: 2.0 kg at radius 50 mm, angle 0 degrees, axial position 0.0 m
  Plane B: 3.0 kg at radius 40 mm, angle 60 degrees, axial position 0.15 m
Correction planes:
  Plane C: radius 60 mm, axial position 0.05 m
  Plane D: radius 50 mm, axial position 0.25 m

Results:
  Correction C: 2.725 kg at 198.5 degrees
  Correction D: 1.044 kg at 264.5 degrees
  Both post-correction residuals < 1e-9


Example 2: Three-Cylinder Inline Engine (120 degree spacing)

Axial positions: 0.0, 0.1, 0.2 m
Reference plane at 0.0 m
Speed: 100 rad/s

Results:
  Primary force: BALANCED (resultant = 0)
  Secondary force: BALANCED (resultant = 0)
  Primary moment: UNBALANCED (resultant MRx = 0.004330 kg-m^2)
  Secondary moment: UNBALANCED


Example 3: Flywheel Sizing
---------------------------
Torque variation: T(theta) = 500 + 300*sin(theta) over one revolution (2*pi)
Mean operating speed: 300 rev/min
Target coefficient of fluctuation of speed: 0.05

Results:
  Mean torque: 500.0 Nm
  Energy per cycle: 3141.6 J
  Maximum energy fluctuation: 600.0 J
  Required flywheel inertia: 12.159 kg-m^2


Example 4: Two-Cylinder Opposed Engine (180 degree)
-----------------------------------------------------
