PROTOCOL SYNOPSIS

Study Number: XYZ2024

Title: A Phase II, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Evaluate
the Efficacy and Safety of Antihypertensin-X 10 mg in Patients with Essential Hypertension

Phase: Phase II

Design: Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group

Indication: Essential Hypertension

Treatment Arms:
  - Arm 1 (ARMCD: TRTX10): Antihypertensin-X 10 mg oral tablet once daily
  - Arm 2 (ARMCD: PBO): Matching Placebo oral tablet once daily

Study Duration: 8 weeks treatment period

Planned Number of Subjects: 60 (30 per arm)

Planned Visits:
  - Visit 1 (Screening): Day -14
  - Visit 2 (Baseline/Randomization): Day 1
  - Visit 3 (Week 2): Day 15
  - Visit 4 (Week 4): Day 29
  - Visit 5 (Week 8 / End of Study): Day 57

Study Start Date: January 15, 2024
Study End Date: April 30, 2024

Sponsor: PharmaCorp Inc.
Regulatory Activity: IND 123456

Inclusion Criteria:
  - Adults aged 18 years and older
  - Diagnosed with essential hypertension
  - Systolic blood pressure >= 140 mmHg at screening

Exclusion Criteria:
  - Secondary hypertension
  - Severe renal impairment
  - Pregnant or lactating women
