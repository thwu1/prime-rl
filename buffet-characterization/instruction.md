Experimental wind tunnel data from the ONERA OAT15A supercritical airfoil is available at `/app/data/`. Test conditions: Mach 0.73, Re_c = 3×10⁶, T_static = 271 K, chord = 230 mm. Six angles of attack were tested spanning pre-buffet through developed buffet regimes.

Data files (Tecplot ASCII format):

- `/app/data/cp_distributions.dat` — Surface pressure coefficient distributions
- `/app/data/pressure_spectra.dat` — Unsteady surface pressure power spectral density
- `/app/data/dpw8_submission_template.dat` — DPW-8 data submission format reference

Produce these deliverables:

**`/app/results/shock_positions.csv`** — Header: `alpha,x_shock,delta_cp`. The normal shock chordwise position on the upper surface and the pressure coefficient magnitude across the shock for each angle of attack.

**`/app/results/section_loads.csv`** — Header: `alpha,cl,cm_qc`. Section lift coefficient and pitching moment coefficient about the quarter-chord for each condition, derived from the measured surface pressures.

**`/app/results/spectral_analysis.csv`** — Header: `alpha,f_dominant_hz,strouhal,psd_peak,narrowband_fraction`. The dominant unsteady pressure frequency, its non-dimensional form using the freestream conditions and chord length, the peak spectral amplitude, and the fraction of spectral energy concentrated near the dominant frequency.

**`/app/results/buffet_onset.json`** — JSON containing `onset_alpha_deg` (float) and `classification` (object mapping each tested angle as a string key to `"pre-buffet"` or `"buffet"`).

**`/app/results/summary.tec`** — Tecplot ASCII format file with `TITLE`, `VARIABLES` (including at minimum `ALPHA`, `CL`, `CM_QC`, `X_SHOCK`, `DELTA_CP`, `F_BUFFET`, `STROUHAL`), and `ZONE` headers containing the consolidated analysis results per DPW-8 conventions.

**`/app/plots/cp_overlay.png`** — All six Cp distributions overlaid with upper and lower surfaces distinguishable. Generated by a gnuplot script saved in `/app/plots/`.

**`/app/plots/psd_comparison.png`** — Spectral comparison across all six conditions with logarithmic ordinate. Generated by a gnuplot script saved in `/app/plots/`.
