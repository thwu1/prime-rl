RATE SPREAD ENRICHMENT SPECIFICATION
======================================
(Derived from CFPB HMDA Rate Spread methodology and Fed G.19 data)

For each loan processed through the APR calculator, compute the
following enrichment fields for compliance analysis.

1. TERM YEARS
   The loan term expressed as whole years.
   Compute: max(1, round(days_from_consummation_to_term_end / 365.25))
   where term_end is the later of:
     - the date of the last scheduled payment, and
     - the date of the last advance.
   For a payment group, the last payment date is:
     first_date + (count - 1) * period_months months forward.

2. APOR RATE
   Look up the Average Prime Offer Rate from the APOR data file
   (apor_fixed.dat). Match the loan's term_years to the nearest
   available term entry in the file. When equidistant between two
   entries, select the shorter term.

3. RATE SPREAD
   rate_spread = computed_apr - apor_rate
   Round to 2 decimal places.

4. HIGH COST FLAG
   high_cost = 1 if rate_spread > 6.5, otherwise 0.
