Build a working grading engine at `/app/grader.py` for a physics research benchmark. The engine must evaluate code submissions against gold-standard challenge answers and produce a fully correct evaluation report.

Challenge definitions are in `/app/challenges/` and submissions to evaluate are in `/app/submissions/`. The grading specification (data formats, comparison modes, and correctness criteria) is at `/app/GRADING_SPEC.md`.

Run your grader to produce `/app/evaluation_report.json` with correct scores for every submission.
