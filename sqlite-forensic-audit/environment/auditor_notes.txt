WAREHOUSE SYSTEM - EXTERNAL AUDIT PRELIMINARY FINDINGS
=======================================================

During our review of the warehouse management system's financial
reports, we identified several areas of concern that require
immediate investigation and correction:

1. FINANCIAL TOTALS: The reported total inventory value does not
   match our manual calculations. We believe some inventory records
   contain data that should not be included in financial calculations,
   and conversely some valid data may be mishandled by the script.

2. DUPLICATE RECORDS: We observed what appear to be redundant
   inventory entries for certain product-warehouse combinations.
   The report's count does not align with the number of affected
   groups we identified manually.

3. REFERENTIAL INTEGRITY: Some records appear to reference parent
   records that do not exist in the database. The reporting script
   may or may not handle these correctly — verify each check.

4. HIERARCHY ERRORS: The reported category hierarchy depth does
   not match our understanding of the category structure.

5. PRICING INCONSISTENCIES: We noticed discrepancies between product
   pricing stored in different locations within the database. The
   script's detection of these may be incomplete or overcounting.

6. INTERNAL METADATA: Certain database metadata tables appear to
   contain inaccurate or suspicious entries. The existing report
   does not properly evaluate these.

7. CATEGORY BREAKDOWN: The per-category financial breakdown appears
   incomplete. Several categories with known inventory are missing
   entirely from the report output.

8. DATA TYPE CONCERNS: Some columns appear to contain values stored
   in unexpected formats. The script's detection methodology for
   these may be flawed.

The existing reporting script (/app/report.py) was written by a
developer who may not have been fully aware of the database engine's
behavioral characteristics. A thorough investigation of the database
schema, data, and internal state is needed.

NOTE: Not all of the report's values are wrong — some metrics may
be correctly computed. Part of your job is to determine which.
