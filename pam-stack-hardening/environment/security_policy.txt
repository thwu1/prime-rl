PAM Security Policy Requirements
=================================

This document specifies the required security configuration for the server's
PAM (Pluggable Authentication Modules) system. All requirements must be
implemented correctly for the system to pass audit.

1. AUTHENTICATION
   - All user authentication MUST require a valid password.
   - No authentication bypass mechanisms may exist in the PAM stack.
   - The pam_unix module must be the sole password verification method.
   - Control flags must ensure that authentication failure causes denial.

2. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT (pam_faillock)
   - Accounts must be locked after 5 consecutive failed authentication attempts.
   - Locked accounts must automatically unlock after 900 seconds (15 minutes).
   - The root account must NOT be subject to lockout (even_deny_root = false).
   - pam_faillock must be integrated into the auth stack with preauth and authfail.
   - pam_faillock must also be present in the account management stack.
   - Faillock configuration belongs in /etc/security/faillock.conf.

3. PRIVILEGE ESCALATION (su)
   - Only members of the "wheel" group may use su to switch users.
   - The pam_wheel module must enforce this restriction with the use_uid option.
   - pam_wheel must be placed after pam_rootok but before common-auth.

4. ACCESS CONTROL (pam_access)
   - The "restricted" group may only log in from LOCAL terminals.
   - The "svcaccounts" group must be denied login from ALL sources.
   - All other users may log in from any source.
   - pam_access must be included in the account management stack.
   - Rules belong in /etc/security/access.conf.

5. PASSWORD QUALITY (pam_pwquality)
   - Minimum password length: 12 characters.
   - Required character classes: 4 (uppercase, lowercase, digit, special).
   - Individual credit: dcredit=-1, ucredit=-1, lcredit=-1, ocredit=-1.
   - pam_pwquality must be in the password management stack before pam_unix.
   - Configuration belongs in /etc/security/pwquality.conf.

6. RESOURCE LIMITS (pam_limits)
   - @admins group: nproc=unlimited, nofile=65536
   - @developers group: nproc=200, nofile=8192
   - Default (*): nproc=100, nofile=4096
   - pam_limits must be in the session management stack.
   - Limits belong in /etc/security/limits.conf.

7. USERS AND GROUPS (pre-configured, do not modify)
   - admin1: member of admins, wheel groups (password: Admin1Pass)
   - dev1: member of developers group (password: Dev1Pass)
   - restricted1: member of restricted group (password: Restr1Pass)
   - svcaccount: member of svcaccounts group, shell=/usr/sbin/nologin (password: SvcAcct1Pass)
