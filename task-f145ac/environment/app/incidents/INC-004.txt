INCIDENT INC-004
Severity: Critical
Date: 2024-10-25
Reporter: Dispatch Operations

SUMMARY: Duplicate tickets delivered during dispatcher failover

DESCRIPTION:
During scheduled dispatcher maintenance, the primary dispatcher for roads
310-320 disconnected. A backup dispatcher connected moments later and received
tickets that had already been delivered to the primary dispatcher before it
went offline.

Tickets that were queued while no dispatcher was available should be delivered
exactly once — to the first dispatcher that connects for that road. Once
delivered, the queued ticket must be removed from the pending queue.

Impact: Drivers received duplicate violation notices, 47 contested tickets
