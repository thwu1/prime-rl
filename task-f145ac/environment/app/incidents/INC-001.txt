INCIDENT INC-001
Severity: Medium
Date: 2024-10-15
Reporter: Legal Department

SUMMARY: Tickets issued for vehicles traveling at exactly the posted speed limit

DESCRIPTION:
Multiple drivers have contested tickets where their calculated average speed
was exactly equal to the posted limit. Example: Road 1742 with 55 mph limit —
a vehicle was clocked at 55.00 mph and received a ticket.

Legal has confirmed that we may only ticket vehicles that EXCEED the speed
limit. Traveling at exactly the limit is not a violation.

Affected roads: multiple (systemic issue)
