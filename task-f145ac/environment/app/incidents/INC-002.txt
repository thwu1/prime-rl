INCIDENT INC-002
Severity: High
Date: 2024-10-18
Reporter: Network Operations

SUMMARY: Heartbeat message flooding on some client connections

DESCRIPTION:
Monitoring detected clients receiving heartbeat messages at elevated rates.
One dispatcher client reported receiving heartbeats at approximately double
the expected frequency. Issue appears related to clients that reconnect or
adjust their heartbeat preferences during a session.

The protocol allows a client to request a heartbeat interval exactly once
per connection. Subsequent requests should be treated as a protocol error.

Impact: Increased bandwidth usage, client-side log flooding
