INCIDENT INC-005
Severity: High
Date: 2024-10-28
Reporter: Revenue Audit

SUMMARY: Duplicate tickets for violations spanning midnight

DESCRIPTION:
Vehicle plate ABC-1234 received two tickets on the same stretch of road.
The first ticket covered observations from timestamp 86100 (day 0) to
timestamp 86700 (day 1) — spanning the midnight boundary. A second ticket
was then issued for observations entirely within day 1.

The one-ticket-per-plate-per-day rule requires that a spanning ticket
blocks ALL days it covers, including intermediate and end days. In this
case, both day 0 and day 1 should have been marked as used, preventing
any further tickets for this plate on either day.

Impact: Revenue audit flagged 12 duplicate spanning-day tickets in the
last billing cycle
