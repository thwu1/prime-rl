RBAC Policy Requirements for CI/CD Pipeline
============================================

Service Account: ci-deployer
Service Account Namespace: ci-cd

Required Permissions:

1. In namespace "production" (create a Role + RoleBinding):
   - Create, update, and delete Deployments (API group: apps)
   - Create, update, and delete Services (API group: "" i.e. core)
   - Create, update, and delete ConfigMaps (API group: "" i.e. core)
   - Get and list Pods (API group: "" i.e. core)
   - Get Pods/log (API group: "" i.e. core, resource: pods/log)

2. Cluster-wide (create a ClusterRole + ClusterRoleBinding):
   - Create and delete Namespaces (API group: "" i.e. core)

Write all RBAC manifests as YAML files in /app/rbac/.
Each manifest must have the correct apiVersion (rbac.authorization.k8s.io/v1),
kind, metadata, and spec fields.
