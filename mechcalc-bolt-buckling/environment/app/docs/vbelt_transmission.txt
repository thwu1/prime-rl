Engineering Reference: V-Belt Transmission Design
====================================================

Based on MITCalc documentation, ISO 4184, and ANSI/RMA standards.

1. Geometry

   Transmission ratio: i = d2 / d1

   Belt speed: v = pi * d1 * n1 / 60000  [m/s]
   (d1 in mm, n1 in rpm)

   Wrap angles:
     alpha_small = pi - 2 * arcsin((d2 - d1) / (2 * C))  [radians]
     alpha_large = pi + 2 * arcsin((d2 - d1) / (2 * C))  [radians]
     For equal pulleys (d1 = d2), both wrap angles = pi (180 deg).
     Minimum acceptable wrap angle: 90 degrees.

   Belt length:
     L = 2*C + pi/2*(d1 + d2) + (d2 - d1)^2 / (4*C)

2. Power Sizing

   Design power:  P_design = P_nominal * service_factor

   Wrap angle correction factor (for alpha_small < 180 degrees):
     C_wrap = 1.0 - (180 - alpha_small_deg) * 0.00278

   Corrected power per belt:
     P_corrected = (P_base + P_increment) * C_wrap

   Required number of belts:
     z = ceil(P_design / P_corrected)

3. Force Analysis

   Tensile (effective/driving) force per belt:
     Ft = 1000 * P_design / (z * v)  [N]
     where P_design in kW, v in m/s.

   Centrifugal force per belt:
     Fc = m' * v^2  [N]
     where m' is belt mass per unit length [kg/m].
     This force becomes significant at belt speeds above ~20 m/s.

   Belt preload (based on Euler friction equation for V-groove):
     V-belts run in grooves with typical half-angle of 19 degrees.
     The wedging action increases the effective friction coefficient:
       f_eff = f_base / sin(groove_half_angle)
     with f_base = 0.3 (typical rubber on metal).

     Friction factor over wrap:
       f_alpha = f_eff * alpha_small  (alpha_small in radians)

     Preload force:
       F0 = Ft * (exp(f_alpha) + 1) / (2 * (exp(f_alpha) - 1)) + Fc

   Strand forces:
     Tight side:  F1 = F0 + Ft/2
     Slack side:  F2 = F0 - Ft/2

   Total shaft (bearing) load on the smaller pulley:
     The resultant of both belt strand forces acting on the pulley:
       Fs = 2 * F0 * cos((pi - alpha_small) / 2) * z
     where z is number of belts and alpha_small in radians.

4. Pass Criteria

   The wrap angle on the smaller pulley must be at least 90 degrees
   to ensure adequate friction grip and power transmission capability.
