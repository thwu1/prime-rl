Target Device and Capture Setup
================================

Target
------
  - Simulated embedded microcontroller
  - Firmware: AES-128-ECB encryption (standard FIPS-197)
  - First-round SubBytes operates byte-by-byte on the 16-byte state
  - No software countermeasures are implemented

Capture Equipment
-----------------
  - Simulated oscilloscope sampling at 200 MS/s
  - Device clock: 40 MHz (harmonics present in power rail)
  - AC-coupled differential power probe on Vdd rail

Trace Acquisition
-----------------
  - 5000 traces captured with random 16-byte plaintexts
  - 4000 samples per trace
  - Trigger: rising-edge GPIO toggle at approximate start of round computation
  - Clock feedthrough into power measurement creates strong periodic
    components at device clock harmonics

Known Issues
------------
  - Significant inter-trace clock jitter (tens of samples) due to
    asynchronous trigger setup relative to the sampling clock
  - Minor per-operation timing jitter from pipeline stalls
  - Low-frequency voltage drift on the power supply rail
  - Clock harmonics dominate the raw trace amplitude; data-dependent
    leakage is small relative to the clock baseline
