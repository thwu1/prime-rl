Peer Review — GSE53757 Differential Expression Analysis
========================================================

The following discrepancies were noted during quality assessment of the
preliminary results in /opt/analysis/preliminary_results/:

1. FOLD CHANGE INFLATION
   Multi-probe genes systematically show higher fold changes than expected.
   Published data from von Roemeling et al. (2014) report CA9 log2FC of
   approximately 4.5 in ccRCC versus matched normal; the preliminary results
   show a notably higher value. The inflation pattern correlates with probe
   set count per gene: single-probe genes match expected values while
   two-probe genes are consistently inflated. This suggests the method used
   to derive gene-level expression from multiple probes introduces upward
   bias.

2. SIGNIFICANCE THRESHOLD
   Only a handful of genes reach padj < 0.05 in the preliminary results.
   Published analyses of comparable ccRCC datasets with similar sample sizes
   identify hundreds of differentially expressed genes at this threshold. The
   raw (unadjusted) p-values appear biologically plausible, suggesting the
   issue lies in how they are adjusted for multiple comparisons.

3. RANKING BIAS
   The top 20 differentially expressed genes consist entirely of upregulated
   genes. ALDOB, a well-characterized downregulated marker in ccRCC with
   expected |log2FC| > 3.5, does not appear in the top rankings despite
   having a fold change magnitude exceeding several listed genes. The ranking
   criterion appears directionally biased rather than magnitude-based.

4. P-VALUE DISCREPANCIES
   Spot-checking several genes against independent computations in R reveals
   p-value deviations. The discrepancies are most pronounced for genes where
   the tumor group exhibits substantially greater within-group expression
   variability than the normal group, suggesting an assumption about group
   variance structure may not hold.

5. BATCH STRUCTURE
   Samples were processed in two batches (A and B) according to the sample
   characteristics metadata. Verify whether batch-associated technical
   variation is present in the expression data and whether it affects the
   differential expression conclusions.
