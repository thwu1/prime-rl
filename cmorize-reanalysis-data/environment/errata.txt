ERA5syn Data Errata & Technical Notes v2.0
==========================================

Variable-Specific Issues
------------------------

1. t2m (2-metre temperature)
   - Systematic warm bias of +0.5 K across all grid cells and time steps
   - Altitude-dependent representativeness error due to mismatch between model
     orography and true surface elevation; orography difference field
     (z_model - z_true, meters) provided at /data/ancillary/orog_diff.nc
   - ICAO standard atmosphere lapse rate: 6.5 K/km

2. d2m (2-metre dewpoint temperature), sp (Surface pressure)
   - No known data quality issues
   - Provided as inputs for deriving near-surface specific humidity (huss)

3. tp (Total precipitation)
   - Missing data encoded as -9999.0; _FillValue attribute not set in file
   - Values represent monthly accumulated precipitation in meters of water equivalent

4. ssrd (Surface solar radiation downwards)
   - No known data quality issues
   - Values represent monthly accumulated energy in J m-2

General Notes
-------------
- Raw time values are at the start of each month (00:00 UTC on the 1st)
- CMOR time coordinate values should be at the center of the averaging period
- Time bounds should span the full calendar month [month_start, next_month_start]
