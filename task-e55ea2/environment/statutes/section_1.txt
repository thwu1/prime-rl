SECTION 1 — TAX RATE SCHEDULES

Tax shall be computed by applying the following rate schedules to the
taxpayer's taxable income. Each bracket applies its rate only to the portion
of income within that bracket. The final tax shall be truncated to a whole
dollar amount (round down).

(a) Single:
    If taxable income is:               The tax is:
    Not over $11,600                     10% of taxable income
    Over $11,600 but not over $47,150    $1,160 + 12% of the excess over $11,600
    Over $47,150 but not over $100,525   $5,426 + 22% of the excess over $47,150
    Over $100,525 but not over $191,950  $17,168.50 + 24% of the excess over $100,525
    Over $191,950 but not over $243,725  $39,110.50 + 32% of the excess over $191,950
    Over $243,725 but not over $609,350  $55,678.50 + 35% of the excess over $243,725
    Over $609,350                        $183,647.25 + 37% of the excess over $609,350

(b) Married Filing Jointly and Qualifying Surviving Spouse:
    If taxable income is:               The tax is:
    Not over $23,200                     10% of taxable income
    Over $23,200 but not over $94,300    $2,320 + 12% of the excess over $23,200
    Over $94,300 but not over $201,050   $10,852 + 22% of the excess over $94,300
    Over $201,050 but not over $383,900  $34,337 + 24% of the excess over $201,050
    Over $383,900 but not over $487,450  $78,221 + 32% of the excess over $383,900
    Over $487,450 but not over $731,200  $111,357 + 35% of the excess over $487,450
    Over $731,200                        $196,669.50 + 37% of the excess over $731,200

(c) Head of Household:
    If taxable income is:               The tax is:
    Not over $16,550                     10% of taxable income
    Over $16,550 but not over $63,100    $1,655 + 12% of the excess over $16,550
    Over $63,100 but not over $100,500   $7,241 + 22% of the excess over $63,100
    Over $100,500 but not over $191,950  $15,469 + 24% of the excess over $100,500
    Over $191,950 but not over $243,700  $37,417 + 32% of the excess over $191,950
    Over $243,700 but not over $609,350  $53,977 + 35% of the excess over $243,700
    Over $609,350                        $181,954.50 + 37% of the excess over $609,350

(d) Married Filing Separately:
    If taxable income is:               The tax is:
    Not over $11,600                     10% of taxable income
    Over $11,600 but not over $47,150    $1,160 + 12% of the excess over $11,600
    Over $47,150 but not over $100,525   $5,426 + 22% of the excess over $100,525
    Over $100,525 but not over $191,950  $17,168.50 + 24% of the excess over $100,525
    Over $191,950 but not over $243,725  $39,110.50 + 32% of the excess over $191,950
    Over $243,725 but not over $365,600  $55,678.50 + 35% of the excess over $243,725
    Over $365,600                        $98,334.75 + 37% of the excess over $365,600
