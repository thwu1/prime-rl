SECTION 62 — ADJUSTED GROSS INCOME DEFINED

(a) Adjusted gross income (AGI) shall be equal to gross income (as defined in
Section 61) minus the following above-the-line deductions:
  (1) Student loan interest: the amount of interest paid by the taxpayer
      during the taxable year on any qualified education loan, not to exceed
      $2,500;
  (2) Educator expenses: in the case of an eligible educator, expenses paid
      or incurred by the taxpayer in connection with books, supplies, and
      other classroom materials, not to exceed $300.


SECTION 63 — TAXABLE INCOME DEFINED

(a) In general. — Taxable income shall be computed as follows:
    Taxable income = AGI - Deductions - Personal Exemptions
    If the result is less than zero, taxable income shall be zero.

(b) Deductions. — The deduction amount shall be the greater of:
  (1) The applicable standard deduction (see paragraph (c)); or
  (2) The total amount of the taxpayer's allowable itemized deductions.

(c) Standard deduction amounts. — The standard deduction shall be the sum of
the basic standard deduction and any additional standard deduction:

  (1) Basic standard deduction:
      (A) Married filing jointly or qualifying surviving spouse: $29,200
      (B) Head of household: $21,900
      (C) Single or married filing separately: $14,600

  (2) Additional standard deduction for age or blindness:
      An additional amount shall be allowed for each of the following
      conditions met by the taxpayer (and, if filing jointly, the taxpayer's
      spouse):
      (A) The individual has attained age 65 before the close of the taxable
          year;
      (B) The individual is blind.
      The additional amount is:
      — $1,950 if the individual's filing status is single or head of
        household;
      — $1,550 if the individual's filing status is married filing jointly,
        married filing separately, or qualifying surviving spouse.
