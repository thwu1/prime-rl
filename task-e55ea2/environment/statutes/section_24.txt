SECTION 24 — CHILD TAX CREDIT

(a) Allowance of credit. — There shall be allowed as a credit against the tax
imposed by Section 1 an amount equal to $2,000 for each qualifying child of
the taxpayer.

(b) Qualifying child. — For purposes of this section, a qualifying child is
an individual who:
  (1) is a qualifying child of the taxpayer as defined in Section 152(c); and
  (2) has not attained the age of 17 as of the close of the taxable year.

(c) Limitation based on adjusted gross income. — The amount of the credit
under paragraph (a) shall be reduced (but not below zero) by $50 for each
$1,000 (or fraction thereof) by which the taxpayer's adjusted gross income
exceeds the applicable threshold amount.

(d) Threshold amounts. — The applicable threshold amounts are:
  (1) $400,000 in the case of married individuals filing jointly or a
      qualifying surviving spouse;
  (2) $200,000 in the case of all other filing statuses.

(e) Non-refundable credit. — The credit determined under this section shall
not exceed the tax imposed by Section 1 for the taxable year. That is, the
credit cannot reduce the taxpayer's tax liability below zero.
