SECTION 61 — GROSS INCOME DEFINED

(a) General definition. — Gross income means all income from whatever source
derived, including (but not limited to) the following items:
  (1) Compensation for services, including wages, salaries, fees, and
      commissions;
  (2) Gross income derived from business;
  (3) Gains derived from dealings in property;
  (4) Interest;
  (5) Dividends;
  (6) Rents.

(b) Exclusions from gross income. — The following items shall NOT be included
in gross income:
  (1) The value of property acquired by gift or inheritance (Section 102);
  (2) Amounts received under life insurance contracts by reason of the death
      of the insured (Section 101);
  (3) Employer-provided educational assistance (Section 127): the first
      $5,250 of amounts paid or expenses incurred by the employer for
      educational assistance to the employee shall be excluded from gross
      income. Any amount exceeding $5,250 shall be included in gross income.
