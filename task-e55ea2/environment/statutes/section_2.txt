SECTION 2 — FILING STATUS

A taxpayer's filing status for the taxable year shall be determined according
to the following rules, applied in the order listed. The first status whose
conditions are satisfied shall be the taxpayer's filing status.

(a) Qualifying Surviving Spouse. — An individual shall be treated as a
qualifying surviving spouse if all of the following conditions are met:
  (1) The individual's spouse died during either of the two taxable years
      immediately preceding the current taxable year (not in the current
      taxable year itself);
  (2) The individual has not remarried before the close of the current taxable
      year;
  (3) The individual maintains a household which, for the taxable year, is the
      principal place of abode of a son, stepson, daughter, or stepdaughter of
      the individual who is a dependent of the individual (as defined in
      Section 152).
A qualifying surviving spouse shall use the standard deduction and tax rate
schedule applicable to married individuals filing jointly.

(b) Married Filing Jointly. — An individual shall file as married filing
jointly if:
  (1) The individual is legally married as of the last day of the taxable
      year; and
  (2) The individual and the individual's spouse both elect to file a joint
      return.

(c) Married Filing Separately. — An individual shall file as married filing
separately if:
  (1) The individual is legally married as of the last day of the taxable
      year; and
  (2) The individual does not elect to file jointly with the individual's
      spouse.

(d) Head of Household. — An individual shall file as head of household if:
  (1) The individual is not married as of the last day of the taxable year;
  (2) The individual is not a qualifying surviving spouse; and
  (3) The individual maintains a household which, for more than one-half of
      the taxable year, is the principal place of abode of—
      (A) a qualifying child of the taxpayer (as defined in Section 152(c));
          or
      (B) a qualifying relative (as defined in Section 152(d)) who is the
          taxpayer's father or mother; provided that in the case of a
          dependent parent who does not share the taxpayer's abode, the
          taxpayer pays more than one-half the cost of maintaining the
          parent's separate household.

(e) Single. — An individual who does not qualify under any of paragraphs (a)
through (d) shall file as single.
