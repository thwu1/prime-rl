EXTRACT FROM BASEL FRAMEWORK — MAR21 & MAR22
Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk (d457, January 2019)
================================================================

This file contains relevant extracts from the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision
standard d457 for implementing the Sensitivities-Based Method (SBM) and Default Risk
Capital (DRC) requirements.


MAR21 — SENSITIVITIES-BASED METHOD (SBM)
-----------------------------------------

MAR21.1  The sensitivities-based method capital requirement is the sum of three
risk charges: delta, vega, and curvature. This portfolio requires only the delta
risk charge computation.

MAR21.4  Delta risk capital requirement aggregation

  (1) For each risk factor k within instrument i, compute the net sensitivity s_{k,i}.

  (2) Compute the weighted sensitivity:
        WS_k = RW_k * s_k
      where RW_k is the prescribed risk weight for risk factor k, and s_k is the
      net sensitivity.

  (3) For risk classes where a specified currency/pair reduction applies and the
      portfolio configuration enables it, the risk weight is divided by sqrt(2).

  (4) WITHIN-BUCKET aggregation: For each bucket b, the bucket-level capital
      K_b is:
        K_b = sqrt( max(0, sum_k(WS_k^2) + sum_{k!=l}(rho_{kl} * WS_k * WS_l) ) )
      where rho_{kl} is the prescribed intra-bucket correlation between risk
      factors k and l within the same bucket.

      EXCEPTION: For equity "other sector" bucket (bucket 11) and CSR non-sec
      residual bucket (bucket 16), K_b = sum of absolute values of WS_k.

  (5) ACROSS-BUCKET aggregation: The risk class-level capital is:
        Capital = sqrt( sum_b(K_b^2) + sum_{b!=c}(gamma_{bc} * S_b * S_c) )
      where S_b = sum_k(WS_k) for bucket b, and gamma_{bc} is the prescribed
      cross-bucket correlation.

      (a) For FX risk, each currency is its own bucket, and K_b = |WS| for a
          single-position bucket.

      (b) If the expression under the square root is negative, use the
          ALTERNATIVE S_b specification:
            S_b = max(min(S_b, K_b), -K_b)
          and recompute the expression.


MAR21.5  Three correlation scenarios

  The delta risk capital requirement must be computed under three correlation
  scenarios:

  (a) MEDIUM correlation: Use the prescribed correlation values directly.

  (b) HIGH correlation: Multiply ALL intra-bucket correlations rho_{kl} and
      cross-bucket correlations gamma_{bc} by 1.25, capping each at 1.0:
        rho_high = min(1.25 * rho_medium, 1.0)
        gamma_high = min(1.25 * gamma_medium, 1.0)

  (c) LOW correlation: Replace each correlation with:
        rho_low = max(2 * rho_medium - 1, 0.75 * rho_medium)
        gamma_low = max(2 * gamma_medium - 1, 0.75 * gamma_medium)

  The delta risk capital requirement is the MAXIMUM of the three scenario totals.
  Each scenario total is the simple sum of risk class-level capitals.


RISK CLASS: EQUITY (MAR21.40-MAR21.52)
--------------------------------------

  Risk factors: Each equity issuer is a distinct risk factor.
  Buckets: Based on market cap (large/small), economy (emerging/developed), and
  sector. See equity_risk_weights.tsv and equity_intra_corr.tsv.
  Bucket 11: "Other sector" — uses sum-of-absolute-values aggregation.
  Cross-bucket: See equity_cross_corr.tsv.


RISK CLASS: FX (MAR21.78-MAR21.83)
-----------------------------------

  Risk factors: Each currency vs. reporting currency is a separate risk factor.
  Each currency pair is its own bucket with a single risk factor.
  K_b = |WS_k| (absolute value of weighted sensitivity).
  All cross-bucket correlations use a single gamma parameter.
  See fx_params.tsv.


RISK CLASS: GIRR (MAR21.14-MAR21.25)
-------------------------------------

  Risk factors: For each currency (bucket), risk factors are defined at
  prescribed tenors along the yield curve, plus inflation and cross-currency
  basis risk factors.

  Correlation between yield curve tenors k and l on the SAME curve:
    rho_{kl} = max( exp(-theta * |T_k - T_l| / min(T_k, T_l)), floor )
  where theta and floor are in girr_correlation.tsv.

  Correlation between same tenor, DIFFERENT curves:
    rho = same_tenor_different_curve parameter

  Correlation between different tenor, DIFFERENT curves:
    rho = (tenor correlation from above) * different_tenor_different_curve_factor

  Inflation risk factor correlation to yield curve tenors:
    rho = inflation_to_yield_curve parameter

  Cross-currency basis risk factor correlation to ALL other risk factors:
    rho = xccy_basis_to_any parameter (= 0)

  Specified currencies: For currencies in the specified list, risk weights are
  divided by the specified_currency_rw_divisor (= sqrt(2)).

  Cross-bucket (across currencies): gamma from girr_correlation.tsv.


RISK CLASS: CSR NON-SECURITISATION (MAR21.53-MAR21.63)
------------------------------------------------------

  Risk factors: Each credit spread curve (issuer x tenor x curve type).
  Buckets: By sector and credit quality (IG vs HY/NR).
  See csr_nonsec_risk_weights.tsv.

  Intra-bucket correlation: Product of three factors:
    rho_{kl} = rho_name * rho_tenor * rho_basis
  where each factor depends on whether the two risk factors share the same
  issuer name, tenor, and curve basis respectively.
  See csr_nonsec_intra_corr.tsv.
  Bucket 16: "Other sector" — uses sum-of-absolute-values aggregation.

  Cross-bucket correlation: Product of rating factor and sector factor.
    gamma_{bc} = rating_factor * sector_factor
  See csr_nonsec_cross_corr.tsv.
  Bucket 16 to any: gamma = 0.


MAR22 — DEFAULT RISK CAPITAL (DRC) FOR EQUITY (MAR22.23-22.25)
--------------------------------------------------------------

  (1) Compute Jump-to-Default (JTD) positions: For equities, JTD = market value
      (sensitivity). Positive = long, negative = short.

  (2) Apply credit-rating-based risk weights from drc_equity_risk_weights.tsv.

  (3) Compute the Hedge Benefit Ratio (HBR):
        HBR = sum(JTD_long) / (sum(JTD_long) + sum(|JTD_short|))

  (4) Default risk capital:
        DRC = max(0, sum(RW_i * JTD_i for long) - HBR * sum(RW_i * |JTD_i| for short))

  The HBR limits the offset benefit of short positions against long positions.


TOTAL CAPITAL
-------------
  total_capital = sbm_delta_capital + drc_equity

  where sbm_delta_capital = max over three scenarios of (sum of risk class capitals).
