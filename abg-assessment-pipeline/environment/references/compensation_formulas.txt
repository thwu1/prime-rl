COMPENSATION IN ACID-BASE DISORDERS
====================================

The extent of respiratory compensation for a metabolic disorder is determined
by the balance between the abnormality in the pH (the drive to change) and the
work required (e.g. respiratory muscle effort in hyperventilation).

Knowledge of the expected degree of compensation is necessary to determine the
presence of an additional respiratory or metabolic disorder. The direction of
change in the compensating parameter should normalise the pH.

METABOLIC ACIDOSIS — Expected Respiratory Compensation
------------------------------------------------------
The commonest formula in use:

  Expected PaCO2 = (1.5 × [HCO3-]) + 8    (±2)

Alternative: Expected PaCO2 ≈ last two digits of the pH (from pH 7.1 to 7.6,
±2-5 mmHg). Other approaches: expected change in PaCO2 = Standard Base Excess;
or 1.2 mmHg fall in PaCO2 for each 1 mmol/L reduction in HCO3.

If measured PaCO2 is more than the expected PaCO2 + tolerance, there is an
additional respiratory acidosis. If measured PaCO2 is less than expected
PaCO2 - tolerance, there is an additional respiratory alkalosis.

METABOLIC ALKALOSIS — Expected Respiratory Compensation
-------------------------------------------------------
  Expected PaCO2 = (0.7 × [HCO3-]) + 20    (±5)

The reliability may be less than with metabolic acidosis. Other approaches:
change in PaCO2 = 0.6 × Standard Base Excess; 0.7 mmHg rise in PaCO2 for each
1 mmol/L increase in HCO3.

RESPIRATORY ACIDOSIS — Expected Metabolic Compensation (1-2-3-4-5 Rule)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Acute:
  Expected HCO3 = 24 + 1 × (PaCO2 - 40) / 10

Chronic:
  Expected HCO3 = 24 + 4 × (PaCO2 - 40) / 10

Tolerance: ±3

Metabolic compensation begins within 5-10 minutes (whole body buffering) but
requires 3-5 days to complete with further renal processes.

If measured HCO3 < expected - 3 → additional metabolic acidosis
If measured HCO3 > expected + 3 → additional metabolic alkalosis

RESPIRATORY ALKALOSIS — Expected Metabolic Compensation (1-2-3-4-5 Rule)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Acute:
  Expected HCO3 = 24 - 2 × (40 - PaCO2) / 10

Chronic:
  Expected HCO3 = 24 - 5 × (40 - PaCO2) / 10

Tolerance: ±3

Respiratory compensation occurs quickly (within 30 minutes, complete within
12-24 hours).

Source: LITFL Critical Care Compendium — Respiratory Compensation in Metabolic
Disorders & Blood Gas Interpretation (Chris Nickson, Jeremy Rogers, Mike Cadogan)
