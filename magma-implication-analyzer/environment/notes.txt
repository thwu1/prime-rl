Implication Analysis Notes
==========================
Date: November 2024
Method: Exhaustive enumeration of magma operation tables

Orders checked: 1 (1 table), 2 (16 tables), 3 (19683 tables)
Total tables examined: 19700

Methodology:
For each pair (E_i, E_j), checked whether every magma satisfying E_i
also satisfies E_j. If so, recorded E_i => E_j.

Known issues and assumptions:

1. The "dual" equation pairs (E6/E7 and E8/E9) share structural
   similarities — E6 is x*(x*y) = x*y and E7 is (x*y)*y = x*y,
   which are left-right mirrors. Similarly for E8/E9. After reviewing
   the algebraic structure, these pairs should be equivalent by
   left-right duality of the binary operation. Both directions marked
   as implications without exhaustive verification.

2. Commutativity (E4) is a reordering property while associativity (E5)
   is a regrouping property. Since reordering is arguably "stronger"
   (it changes which element is being operated on, not just grouping),
   E4 => E5 was marked. This aligns with the intuition that commutative
   structures tend to be associative.

3. Idempotency (E3) is closely related to the iterated idempotent law
   (E6): if x*x = x for all x, then in x*(x*y), the outer x is being
   "absorbed" by the idempotent property. Marked E3 => E6.

4. The projection laws E1 and E2 are the strongest laws — they trivialize
   the operation. Full verification of all E1/E2 implications was
   truncated due to computational budget constraints during the order-3
   sweep. Some implications from E1/E2 to other equations may be
   missing from the matrix.

5. Associativity (E5) clearly implies its special cases E8 and E9
   (substitute repeated variables into the associativity equation).
   The flexible law E10 has a different variable pattern — (x*y)*x vs
   x*(y*x) — which is NOT a direct variable substitution of E5.
   Left as non-implication pending further analysis.

Status: Preliminary. Known to have gaps in projection-law checks.
Dual-pair reasoning should be double-checked against actual models.
