QUALIFIED DIVIDENDS AND CAPITAL GAIN TAX WORKSHEET -- LINE 16

Use this worksheet to figure the tax on Form 1040, line 16, if:
  (a) Form 1040, line 3a (qualified dividends) is more than zero, OR
  (b) You are filing Schedule D AND both Schedule D, line 15 and
      line 16 are more than zero.

If NEITHER condition applies, do not use this worksheet. Instead,
figure your tax using the Tax Rate Schedules only.

CAUTION: If your taxable income (Form 1040, line 15) is zero, your
tax is zero. Do not complete this worksheet.

Before you begin, make sure you have completed Schedule D if required.

Worksheet Lines:

 1. Enter the amount from Form 1040, line 15 (taxable income).

 2. Enter the amount from Form 1040, line 3a (qualified dividends).

 3. If you are filing Schedule D, enter the smaller of Schedule D,
    line 15, or Schedule D, line 16. If either is zero or less,
    enter 0 on this line.
    If you are NOT filing Schedule D, enter 0.

 4. Add lines 2 and 3.

 5. Subtract line 4 from line 1. If zero or less, enter 0.

 6. Enter:
      $48,350 if Single or Married Filing Separately
      $96,700 if Married Filing Jointly or Qualifying Surviving Spouse
      $64,750 if Head of Household

 7. Enter the smaller of line 1 or line 6.

 8. Enter the smaller of line 5 or line 7.

 9. Subtract line 8 from line 7. This amount is taxed at 0%.

10. Enter the smaller of line 1 or line 4.

11. Enter the amount from line 9.

12. Subtract line 11 from line 10.

13. Enter:
      $533,400 if Single
      $600,050 if Married Filing Jointly or Qualifying Surviving Spouse
      $300,000 if Married Filing Separately
      $566,700 if Head of Household

14. Enter the smaller of line 1 or line 13.

15. Add lines 5 and 9.

16. Subtract line 15 from line 14. If zero or less, enter 0.

17. Enter the smaller of line 12 or line 16.

18. Multiply line 17 by 15% (0.15).

19. Add lines 9 and 17.

20. Subtract line 19 from line 10.

21. Multiply line 20 by 20% (0.20).

22. Figure the tax on the amount on line 5. Use the Tax Rate
    Schedules for your filing status.

23. Add lines 18, 21, and 22.

24. Figure the tax on the amount on line 1. Use the Tax Rate
    Schedules for your filing status.

25. Tax. Enter the smaller of line 23 or line 24 here and on
    Form 1040, line 16.
