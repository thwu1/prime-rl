SCHEDULE H (FORM 1040) -- HOUSEHOLD EMPLOYMENT TAXES -- TAX YEAR 2025

Use Schedule H if you paid cash wages to a household employee and
the wages were subject to social security, Medicare, or federal
income tax withholding.

Threshold: If you paid any one household employee cash wages of
$2,800 or more in 2025, you must file Schedule H.

PART I -- Social Security, Medicare, and Federal Income Taxes

Line 1 -- Total cash wages subject to social security tax.
  Enter the total cash wages you paid to all household employees
  that are subject to social security tax.

Line 2 -- Social security tax. Multiply line 1 by 12.4% (0.124).
  Round to the nearest whole dollar.

Line 3 -- Total cash wages subject to Medicare tax.
  Enter the total cash wages you paid to all household employees
  that are subject to Medicare tax.

Line 4 -- Medicare tax. Multiply line 3 by 2.9% (0.029).
  Round to the nearest whole dollar.

Line 5 -- Total cash wages subject to Additional Medicare Tax
  withholding. Enter the amount of wages subject to Additional
  Medicare Tax withholding. This applies only to wages paid in
  excess of the applicable threshold.

Line 6 -- Additional Medicare Tax withholding. Multiply line 5
  by 0.9% (0.009). Round to the nearest whole dollar.

Line 7 -- Federal income tax withheld, if any. Enter the total
  federal income tax you withheld from your household employee(s)
  wages during the year, if applicable.

Line 8 -- Total social security, Medicare, and federal income
  taxes. Add lines 2, 4, 6, and 7. Enter this total.

REPORTING: Enter the amount from Schedule H, line 8, on
Schedule 2 (Form 1040), line 9.
