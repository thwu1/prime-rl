SCHEDULE 2 (FORM 1040) -- ADDITIONAL TAXES -- TAX YEAR 2025

Part I -- Tax

Line 1z -- Additions to tax (excess advance premium tax credit
  repayment, etc.). If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 2 -- Alternative minimum tax from Form 6251. If not applicable,
  enter 0.

Line 3 -- Add lines 1z and 2. Enter here and on Form 1040, line 17.

Part II -- Other Taxes

Line 4 -- Self-employment tax from Schedule SE. If not applicable,
  enter 0.

Lines 5 through 8 -- Various additional taxes (unreported tip income
  tax, uncollected SS/Medicare, additional tax on IRAs, etc.). If not
  applicable, enter 0 for each.

Line 9 -- Household employment taxes. Enter the amount from
  Schedule H, line 8. If you did not have household employees,
  enter 0.

Lines 10 through 16 -- Various other taxes (additional Medicare tax,
  net investment income tax, etc.). If not applicable, enter 0 for
  each.

Line 18 -- Total additional taxes from lines 17a through 17z.
  If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 21 -- Total other taxes. Add lines 4 through 16 and line 18.
  Enter here and on Form 1040, line 23.

  For taxpayers whose only other tax is household employment taxes,
  line 21 equals line 9.


SCHEDULE 3 (FORM 1040) -- ADDITIONAL CREDITS AND PAYMENTS -- TY 2025

Part I -- Nonrefundable Credits

Line 1 -- Foreign tax credit from Form 1116. If not applicable,
  enter 0.

Line 2 -- Credit for child and dependent care expenses from
  Form 2441. If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 3 -- Education credits from Form 8863. If not applicable,
  enter 0.

Line 4 -- Retirement savings contributions credit from Form 8880.
  If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 5a -- Residential clean energy credit from Form 5695, line 15.
  If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 5b -- Energy efficient home improvement credit from Form 5695,
  line 32.

Line 7 -- Total other nonrefundable credits (sum of lines 6a
  through 6z). If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 8 -- Add lines 1 through 5a, 5b, and 7. Enter here and on
  Form 1040, line 20. This is the total of your nonrefundable
  credits.
