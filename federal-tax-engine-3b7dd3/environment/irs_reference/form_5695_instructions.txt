FORM 5695 -- RESIDENTIAL ENERGY CREDITS -- TAX YEAR 2025

PART II -- Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit

The credit for qualifying energy improvements is generally 30% of
the cost, subject to the per-category and aggregate limits below.

SECTION A -- Qualified Energy Efficiency Improvements

Each qualifying improvement category has its own per-category credit
limit. Compute 30% (0.30) of the cost for each category and apply
the corresponding maximum.

Line 18b -- Insulation material or air sealing system.
  Multiply cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $1,200.

Line 19h -- Exterior doors meeting applicable Energy Star
  requirements. Multiply total door cost by 30% (0.30).
  Maximum credit: $500.

Line 20d -- Windows and skylights meeting Energy Star certification.
  Multiply total cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $600.

Line 22d -- Central air conditioners meeting applicable standards.
  Multiply cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $600.

Line 23d -- Natural gas, propane, or oil water heaters.
  Multiply cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $600.

Line 24d -- Natural gas, propane, or oil furnace or hot water boiler.
  Multiply cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $600.

Line 25e -- Electrical panel improvements (panelboards, sub-panels,
  branch circuits, or feeders that enable qualified energy property).
  Multiply cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $600.

Line 26c -- Home energy audits conducted by a certified home energy
  auditor. Multiply cost by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $150.

Line 27 -- Add lines 18b, 19h, 20d, 22d, 23d, 24d, 25e, and 26c.

Line 28 -- Enter the SMALLER of line 27 or $1,200. This is the
  annual aggregate limit for all qualified energy efficiency
  improvements in Section A combined. Even if individual line items
  sum to more than $1,200, the total Section A credit cannot exceed
  $1,200 per year.

SECTION B -- Residential Energy Property Expenditures

Line 29 -- Heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, and biomass stoves
  and boilers. These items are in a separate credit group.

Line 29h -- Add the costs of all qualifying electric or natural gas
  heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, and biomass stoves and boilers.
  Multiply the combined total by 30% (0.30). Maximum credit: $2,000.
  This limit is separate from and in addition to the $1,200 Section A
  aggregate limit.

Line 30 -- Add lines 28 and 29h. This is the total energy efficient
  home improvement credit before applying the tax liability limitation.

ENERGY EFFICIENT HOME IMPROVEMENT CREDIT LIMIT WORKSHEET

Use this worksheet to determine the maximum credit you can claim
based on your tax liability.

Step 1. Enter the amount from Form 1040, line 18 (tax plus any
  additions from Schedule 2, Part I).

Step 2. Enter the total of any nonrefundable credits claimed on
  Form 1040, line 19 (child tax credit, credit for other dependents).
  If none, enter 0.

Step 3. Subtract Step 2 from Step 1. If the result is zero or less,
  enter 0. This is your credit limitation amount.

Enter the result from Step 3 on Form 5695, line 31.

Line 31 -- Limitation based on tax liability (from the Credit Limit
  Worksheet above).

Line 32 -- Energy efficient home improvement credit. Enter the SMALLER
  of line 30 or line 31.

  Also include this amount on Schedule 3 (Form 1040), line 5b.
