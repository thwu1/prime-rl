SCHEDULE D (FORM 1040) -- CAPITAL GAINS AND LOSSES -- TAX YEAR 2025

Use Schedule D to report sales or exchanges of capital assets.

PART I -- Short-Term Capital Gains and Losses
(Assets held one year or less)

Line 7 -- Net short-term capital gain or (loss). Combine all
  short-term capital gains and losses. Enter the net result. A net
  loss is entered as a negative number.

PART II -- Long-Term Capital Gains and Losses
(Assets held more than one year)

Line 15 -- Net long-term capital gain or (loss). Combine all
  long-term capital gains and losses. Enter the net result. A net
  loss is entered as a negative number.

PART III -- Summary

Line 16 -- Combine lines 7 and 15. Enter the result.

  If line 16 is a gain, enter this amount on Form 1040, line 7.

  If line 16 is a loss, enter this amount on Form 1040, line 7.
  (For these scenarios, no capital loss deduction limitation is
  required.)

NEXT STEPS: After completing Schedule D, check whether the
Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet applies.
See that worksheet for conditions.
