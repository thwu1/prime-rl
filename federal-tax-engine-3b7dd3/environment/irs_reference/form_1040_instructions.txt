STANDARD DEDUCTION

Most taxpayers have a choice of either taking a standard deduction or
itemizing their deductions. If you do not itemize, you are entitled to
a standard deduction based on your filing status:

  Filing Status                                    Standard Deduction
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  Single or Married Filing Separately              $15,750
  Married Filing Jointly or Qualifying
    Surviving Spouse (QSS)                          $31,500
  Head of Household                                $23,625

LINE INSTRUCTIONS

Line 1a -- Total amount from Form(s) W-2, box 1. Enter the total of
  all wages, salaries, tips, etc. reported on your W-2 form(s). If
  you have multiple W-2 forms, add all box 1 amounts together.

Line 1z -- Add lines 1a through 1h. For most taxpayers with only
  W-2 wage income, this equals line 1a.

Line 2b -- Taxable interest. Enter your total taxable interest from
  Form(s) 1099-INT.

Line 3a -- Qualified dividends. Enter qualified dividends from
  Form(s) 1099-DIV, box 1b. These dividends may qualify for lower
  tax rates. See the Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax
  Worksheet in the separate worksheet instructions.

Line 3b -- Ordinary dividends. Enter total ordinary dividends from
  Form(s) 1099-DIV, box 1a. NOTE: Ordinary dividends (line 3b)
  include qualified dividends (line 3a). Do not subtract qualified
  dividends from ordinary dividends.

Line 7 -- Capital gain or (loss). If you sold or exchanged capital
  assets, complete Schedule D and enter the amount from Schedule D,
  line 16. If you had no capital gain or loss activity, enter 0.

Line 9 -- Total income. Add lines 1z, 2b, 3b, 4b, 5b, 6b, 7, and 8.

Line 10 -- Adjustments to income from Schedule 1, line 26. Enter
  adjustments such as educator expenses, IRA deduction, student loan
  interest deduction, etc. If none, enter 0.

Line 11a -- Adjusted gross income. Subtract line 10 from line 9.
  This is your adjusted gross income (AGI).

Line 12e -- Standard deduction or itemized deductions. Enter your
  standard deduction amount from the table above, or your itemized
  deductions from Schedule A if you choose to itemize.

Line 14 -- Add lines 12e, 13a, and 13b. For most taxpayers without
  qualified business income deduction, this equals line 12e.

Line 15 -- Taxable income. Subtract line 14 from line 11a. If the
  result is zero or less, enter 0.

Line 16 -- Tax. See the separate Tax Rate Schedules to compute your
  tax. If you have qualified dividends (line 3a > 0) or eligible
  capital gains, you may be able to use the Qualified Dividends and
  Capital Gain Tax Worksheet to figure a lower tax. See that
  worksheet for the conditions under which it applies.

Line 17 -- Amount from Schedule 2, line 3. This includes alternative
  minimum tax and other additions. If you have no Schedule 2 Part I
  amounts, enter 0.

Line 18 -- Add lines 16 and 17.

Line 19 -- Child tax credit or credit for other dependents from
  Schedule 8812. If not applicable, enter 0.

Line 20 -- Amount from Schedule 3, line 8. This is the total of your
  nonrefundable credits (including energy credits). If you have no
  Schedule 3 credits, enter 0.

Line 21 -- Add lines 19 and 20.

Line 22 -- Subtract line 21 from line 18. If the result is zero or
  less, enter 0.

Line 23 -- Other taxes, including self-employment tax and household
  employment taxes, from Schedule 2, line 21. If you have no other
  taxes, enter 0.

Line 24 -- Total tax. Add lines 22 and 23.

Line 25a -- Federal income tax withheld from Form(s) W-2, box 2.
  Add all box 2 amounts from your W-2 form(s).

Line 25d -- Total federal income tax withheld. Add lines 25a
  through 25c. For most taxpayers with only W-2 withholding, this
  equals line 25a.

Line 33 -- Total payments. Add lines 25d, 26, and 32. For most
  taxpayers with only W-2 withholding, this equals line 25d.

Line 34 -- Overpaid. If line 33 is more than line 24, subtract
  line 24 from line 33. This is the amount you overpaid (refund).
  If line 33 is not more than line 24, enter 0.

Line 37 -- Amount you owe. If line 24 is more than line 33, subtract
  line 33 from line 24. This is the amount you owe.
  If line 24 is not more than line 33, enter 0.

ROUNDING RULES

All dollar amounts on your return must be rounded to the nearest
whole dollar. For amounts that end in exactly 50 cents, round up
to the next dollar. For example, $1,234.50 rounds to $1,235, and
$1,234.49 rounds to $1,234. This rounding rule applies to all
computations throughout all forms and schedules.
