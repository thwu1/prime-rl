A privacy risk assessment tool is installed at `/app/tools/risk_assessor.py` (run with `--help` to explore its subcommands and options). A compliance certification tool is at `/app/tools/certify.py`.

Investigate the compliance failures, then produce a fully compliant re-anonymization of the original patient data that satisfies all critical MDPS v2.1 controls while maximizing data utility. The risk assessment tool predates the current MDPS revision and may not implement all required metrics — run it against the released dataset, compare its methodology to the compliance specification, and verify correctness before relying on its output.

After producing the compliant dataset, generate a signed compliance certification using the certification tool and populate the `remediated` table in the privacy database with the anonymized records.

Required outputs:

- `/app/output/utility_analysis.json` — containing `weighted_ncp` (float), `per_attribute_ncp` (dict mapping each quasi-identifier to its float NCP), and `num_equivalence_classes` (int)
- `/app/output/certification.json` — signed compliance certification generated by the certification tool with valid HMAC-SHA256 signature
- The `remediated` table in `/app/data/privacy.db` must be populated with the anonymized records matching the CSV output
