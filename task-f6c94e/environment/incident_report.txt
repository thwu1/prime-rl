INCIDENT REPORT #2024-SEC-0147
Priority: HIGH
Status: Open
Reporter: Security Operations Team

Subject: Vulnerability Assessment Pipeline Producing Incorrect Triage Results

During routine cross-validation of our vulnerability assessment pipeline
output against NVD published reference data, critical discrepancies were
identified across multiple output categories.

FINDINGS:

1. SCORE MISMATCH - CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell)
   Pipeline reports base score: 9.0
   NVD published reference score: 10.0
   Note: Log4Shell is a well-documented easily exploitable RCE requiring
   no privileges or user interaction with low attack complexity per NVD.
   A score of 9.0 is inconsistent with these characteristics.

2. MISSING DETECTION - CVE-2014-0160 (Heartbleed)
   OpenSSL 1.0.1f is deployed in our environment as INV-002.
   The pipeline does NOT flag it as affected.
   NVD confirms all OpenSSL versions from 1.0.1 through 1.0.1f
   (inclusive) are vulnerable to this CVE.

3. ENVIRONMENTAL SCORE ANOMALY
   Environmental scores for multiple CVEs do not appear to reflect our
   HIGH confidentiality requirement. Observed scores are consistent with
   default/medium weighting rather than the HIGH setting we configured.
   This affects the prioritization of confidentiality-sensitive
   vulnerabilities across the entire report.

4. RANKING INCONSISTENCY
   The overall remediation ranking does not match expected prioritization.
   Even after accounting for finding #3, environmental scores for certain
   CVEs with Scope=Changed appear inconsistent with CVSS v3.1 expected
   behavior. Multiple entries have incorrect environmental scores.

5. TRAFFIC ANALYSIS INCOMPLETE
   The network traffic analyzer (traffic_analyzer.sh) reports detecting
   only 1 active exploit signature in the captured traffic. Manual packet
   inspection of traffic.pcap suggests at least 3 distinct exploit
   attempts are present (Shellshock, Log4Shell, and path traversal).
   The Shellshock and Log4Shell attack patterns are not being identified
   by the analyzer, causing incorrect active exploitation flagging in the
   final remediation report.

IMPACT: Incorrect vulnerability prioritization may lead to misallocated
remediation resources and unaddressed critical exposures. Missing traffic
correlation means actively exploited vulnerabilities may not be escalated
appropriately.

ACTION REQUIRED: Investigate all pipeline components (database, tool code,
configuration, traffic analysis scripts) and correct all root causes
before next security review.
