# § 101. Definitions
**(a)** In this chapter:

(1) The term “agency” has the meaning given that term in [section 551 of title 5](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section551&num=0&edition=prelim).

(2) The term “covered data” means information that—

  (A) identifies or is linked or reasonably linkable to an individual; and

  (B) is collected, processed, or transferred by means of—

    (i) the internet or any other digital means;

    (ii) a device capable of connecting to the internet; or

    (iii) any sensor or similar technology that collects—

      (I) biometric data;

      (II) geolocation data; or

      (III) health data.
  Such term does not include publicly available information or de-identified data.

(3) The term “Commission” means the Federal Trade Commission.

(4) The term “transfer” means to disclose, release, disseminate, make available, sell, or license covered data, or provide access to covered data, to a third party by any means, including by providing such data to a third party in exchange for consideration, by providing interoperability with a third party, or by otherwise making such data accessible to a third party.

**(b)** Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the Commission under [section 45 of title 15](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section45&num=0&edition=prelim).
