## § 101. Definitions
**(a)** In this chapter:

(1) The term “agency” has the meaning given that term in [section 551 of title 5](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section551&num=0&edition=prelim).

(2) The term “covered data” means information that—

  (A) identifies or is linked or reasonably linkable to an individual; and

  (B) is collected, processed, or transferred by means of—

    (i) the internet or any other digital means;

    (ii) a device capable of connecting to the internet; or

    (iii) any sensor or similar technology that collects—

      (I) biometric data;

      (II) geolocation data; or

      (III) health data.
  Such term does not include publicly available information or de-identified data.

(3) The term “Commission” means the Federal Trade Commission.

(4) The term “transfer” means to disclose, release, disseminate, make available, sell, or license covered data, or provide access to covered data, to a third party by any means, including by providing such data to a third party in exchange for consideration, by providing interoperability with a third party, or by otherwise making such data accessible to a third party.

**(b)** Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit the authority of the Commission under [section 45 of title 15](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section45&num=0&edition=prelim).

### Statutory Notes

#### References in Text
For definition of agency, referred to in subsec. (a)(1), see [section 551 of title 5](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title5-section551&num=0&edition=prelim), Government Organization and Employees.

<a id="section-102"></a>
## § 102. Data collection requirements
**(a)** A covered entity may not collect, process, or transfer covered data unless such collection, processing, or transfer is limited to what is reasonably necessary and proportionate.

**(b)** A covered entity shall obtain affirmative express consent from an individual before—

(1) collecting sensitive covered data from that individual;

(2) transferring covered data to a third party; or

(3) using covered data for targeted advertising directed at the individual.

**(c)** Subsection (b) shall not apply to the collection, processing, or transfer of covered data that is strictly necessary to fulfill a transaction or provide a service specifically requested by the individual.

### Statutory Notes

#### Effective Date
Pub. L. 119–42, § 5, Jan. 15, 2026, 140 Stat. 112, provided that: “This section shall take effect 1 year after the date of enactment of this Act [Jan. 15, 2026].”

#### Regulations
Pub. L. 119–42, § 6, Jan. 15, 2026, 140 Stat. 112, provided that: “Not later than 180 days after the date of enactment of this Act [Jan. 15, 2026], the Commission shall promulgate regulations to carry out this section.”

<a id="section-103"></a>
## § 103. Enforcement
**(a)**

(1) Any covered entity that violates [section 102 of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title99-section102&num=0&edition=prelim) shall be subject to a civil penalty of not more than $50,000 per violation.

(2) The civil penalty under paragraph (1) shall be not more than $100,000 per violation if—

  (A) the violation involved the data of a minor;

  (B) the covered entity had actual knowledge of the violation; or

  (C) the violation resulted in significant harm to an individual.

**(b)** Any individual whose covered data is collected, processed, or transferred in violation of [section 102 of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title99-section102&num=0&edition=prelim) may bring a civil action in any court of competent jurisdiction.

### Notes

2026—Subsec. (a)(1). Pub. L. 119–42, § 8(a), substituted “$50,000” for “$25,000”.

Subsec. (a)(2). Pub. L. 119–42, § 8(b), added par. (2).
